## Which Ship Is Best for Beginners?

The short answer: **build the Sloop first**. It's the ideal balance of speed, cargo space, and combat capability for solo and duo players.

But let's break down all three starter options so you understand *why*.

## Ship Comparison Table

| Ship | Cost | Speed | Cargo | Combat | Best For |
|------|------|-------|-------|--------|----------|
| Skiff | Free (spawn) | ★★★★★ | ★☆☆☆☆ | ★☆☆☆☆ | Exploring only |
| Sloop | 80 Planks, 20 Rope | ★★★★☆ | ★★★☆☆ | ★★★☆☆ | Solo/Duo |
| Brigantine | 200 Planks, 60 Rope, 20 Iron | ★★★☆☆ | ★★★★★ | ★★★★☆ | Crew of 3+ |

## The Skiff – Your Free Starting Vessel

You spawn with a Skiff. It's fast and nimble, but it:
- Has almost **no cargo capacity**
- Sinks in **2–3 cannon hits**
- Can't equip cannons at all

Use the Skiff to explore your starting island and gather resources for your first real ship. **Don't sail far in a Skiff.**

## The Sloop – Best Starter Ship ⭐

The Sloop is the community's consensus best starter ship, and for good reason:

**Advantages:**
- 2 cannon ports (one per side)
- Decent 12-slot cargo hold
- Fast enough to escape most threats
- Can be crewed by 1 player

**How to build it:**
1. Gather **80 Palm Wood Planks** and **20 Fiber Rope**
2. Find a **Shipwright** on any port island
3. Select Sloop → confirm build
4. Takes 2 in-game days to complete

**Recommended Sloop loadout for beginners:**
- Iron Cannons x2
- Repair Kit x3
- Medium Sail (for speed)
- Basic Lantern

## The Brigantine – Wait Until You Have Friends

The Brigantine is a powerful ship but requires a crew of 3+ to operate effectively. Solo, you'll spend more time managing sails than fighting.

It's not a bad ship — it's just not a *beginner* ship.

**When to build a Brigantine:**
- You have 2–3 consistent crewmates
- You've cleared all basic islands solo
- You have enough iron stockpiled (~30+ bars)

## Sloop Build Tips

Once you have your Sloop, prioritize these upgrades:

1. **Reinforced Hull** – adds 40% damage resistance (requires 10 Iron)
2. **Crow's Nest** – spot enemies and islands from further away
3. **Second Anchor** – lets you stay stable in rougher seas
4. **Extra Sail** – boost speed by 20%
