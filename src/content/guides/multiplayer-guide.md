## Windrose Multiplayer Overview

Windrose supports **1–6 players** per ship in co-op mode, plus open-world PvP in designated sea zones. Playing with friends dramatically changes the experience — bigger ships, faster resource gathering, and intense naval battles become possible.

## How to Join a Friend's Game

**Method 1: Direct Invite**
1. Open the main menu → **Friends**
2. Select your friend's name → **Join Ship**
3. You'll spawn at their current anchor point

**Method 2: Port Meeting**
1. Both players sail to the same Port island
2. At the Tavern, select **Crew Up**
3. One player becomes Captain, others join as Crew

**Method 3: Server Code**
1. Captain opens **Ship Log** → **Share Code**
2. Send the 6-digit code to your friend
3. Friend enters code at any Tavern

## Crew Roles Explained

Every ship has defined roles. Assigning roles properly is the difference between a winning crew and chaos.

### Captain
- Controls ship steering
- Makes navigation decisions
- Sets loot rules (shared vs. personal)

### Gunner
- Operates cannons
- Calls out enemy positions
- Manages ammo inventory

### Navigator
- Watches the map
- Tracks weather and wind
- Spots islands and hazards

### Quartermaster (4+ crew)
- Manages cargo hold
- Tracks resources
- Handles trading at ports

## Loot Sharing Settings

The Captain can configure loot rules before sailing:

| Mode | Description |
|------|-------------|
| Equal Split | All loot split evenly |
| Captain Takes All | Cap distributes manually |
| Personal Loot | Each player keeps what they loot |
| Role-Based | Loot assigned by ship role |

> **Recommended for friends:** Equal Split or Role-Based

## Tips for Effective Crew Play

1. **Voice chat is essential** – use Discord while playing
2. **Pre-assign roles before sailing** – don't argue mid-battle
3. **Designate a repair person** – someone should always be patching hull damage
4. **Shared waypoints** – Captain can pin map markers everyone sees
5. **Set a loot goal** – decide what you're farming before you leave port

## PvP Sea Zones

Red zones on the world map are open PvP areas with better loot. 

**Before entering a red zone:**
- Make sure your hull is at 100%
- Everyone should have cannons loaded
- Decide on an escape route if outnumbered
- Bring at least 10 repair kits

## Common Crew Mistakes

- **Firing cannons without calling it** – disrupts steering
- **All crew going ashore** – always leave 1 player on deck
- **Ignoring the weather** – storm zones are deadly for distracted crews
- **No repair kit rotation** – always restock before the next voyage

## FAQ

**Q: Can I join a friend mid-voyage?**  
A: Yes, if the Captain enables it in Ship Settings. You'll spawn on deck.

**Q: What's the max crew size?**  
A: 6 players on a Galleon (the largest ship). Sloops hold 2, Brigantines hold 4.

**Q: Can crew members be kicked?**  
A: Yes — Captain can kick any crew member from the Ship Log menu.
