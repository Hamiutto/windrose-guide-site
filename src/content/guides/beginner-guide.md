## What Is Windrose?

Windrose is a naval exploration and survival game where you captain your own ship, explore uncharted islands, harvest resources, and engage in sea combat. Whether you're sailing solo or with a crew, mastering the basics is essential.

## Your First 10 Minutes

When you first load in, you'll spawn on a small starting island with a basic skiff. Don't panic — here's what to do immediately:

1. **Gather wood** from the trees on shore (press `E` to interact)
2. **Craft basic tools** at the workbench (press `Tab` to open crafting)
3. **Repair your skiff** before setting sail
4. **Check wind direction** — the compass in the top-right shows optimal sailing angles

> **Tip:** Always carry 20+ wood planks before leaving your starting island. Running out of repair materials mid-voyage is the #1 mistake beginners make.

## Understanding Wind

Wind is the core mechanic of Windrose. Your ship moves fastest when sailing **45° off the wind** (called a beam reach). Running directly downwind is slower. Sailing directly into the wind is impossible.

Watch the **wind vane** on your HUD — the green arc shows your optimal sailing zone.

## First Resources to Collect

- **Palm Wood** – basic building and repair material
- **Fiber** – crafting ropes and sails
- **Iron Ore** – found on rocky islands, needed for tools
- **Food** – cook fish to restore hunger

## Surviving Your First Night

Nights in Windrose are dangerous. Sea monsters are more active, and visibility drops significantly.

**Beginner Night Survival Checklist:**
- Anchor in a sheltered bay, not open water
- Light your ship lantern (crafted from oil + glass)
- Keep your hull above 50% integrity
- Never fight sea creatures at night until you have better gear

## Common Beginner Mistakes

1. **Leaving without a compass** – craft one at the workbench before exploring
2. **Ignoring weather** – storm clouds on the horizon mean get to shore
3. **Overloading cargo** – heavy ships move slower and are harder to control
4. **Skipping the tutorial island** – the chests there contain free starter gear

## FAQ

**Q: Can I play Windrose solo?**  
A: Yes! The game is fully playable solo, though some late-game content is designed for crews of 2–4.

**Q: How do I save my progress?**  
A: Windrose saves automatically when you anchor at a port or major island.

**Q: What's the best starting ship?**
